ROLL OR STACK?

How to play

  1. Meet the toyListen to its name and look at the surface facing down.
  2. Choose a stationTap Roll to try the blue slide, or Stack to try the starter block.
  3. Watch what happensThe toy may roll, slide, tumble, rock, tip off, or stay steady.
  4. Turn and try againIn Moderate and Difficult modes, turn supported toys to test another surface.

There is no timer and no wrong answer. Repeat any experiment as many times as you like.
