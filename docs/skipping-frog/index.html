How to Play

  1. Choose a mode, then spin its wheel to select a skip number.
  2. The frog begins on Stone 0. Add the skip number and tap only the next correct stone.
  3. Simple needs 12 correct jumps, Moderate needs 15, and Difficult needs 20.
  4. After either of the first two mistakes, the sinking frog returns to its last correct stone. The third mistake ends the game.
  5. The camera follows the frog and reveals the numbered path through Stone 220.
