How to play

  1. Choose a modePick Simple, Moderate, or Difficult for the player’s age and challenge level.
  2. Spin for a numberThe arrow shows the number for your move. You can spin again when you need another number.
  3. Choose a matching spaceClick an empty space next to the same number in its 3 × 3 block.
  4. Watch the group clearThe game fills connected empty spaces to complete the group. When enough matching numbers connect, they disappear.
  5. Keep goingClear a group to reset the spin counter. Leave one number to win!

Use Start / Reset anytime to begin a fresh board.
