How to Play

  1. Choose a scene and a mode.
  2. Listen to the clue or press Replay to hear it again.
  3. Tap the place described by the clue. In Difficult mode, find two different places in order before the digit is revealed.
  4. Found digits are added to the list below the picture. If a clue is difficult to locate, press Reveal.
  5. Find every digit to make the kitten peek out.
