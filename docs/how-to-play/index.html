How to play

Spin the wheel, place a matching number, and clear connected number groups. Keep going until one lucky number is left!

  1. Choose a modePick the level that feels right. Each mode changes the board size, wheel numbers, and spin chances.
  2. Spin the wheelThe arrow points to the number for your next move. You can spin again if you need another number.
  3. Find a matching spaceChoose an empty cell beside the same number. Only up, down, left, and right neighbors in that 3×3 block count.
  4. Clear a number groupYour number joins the group. When enough matching numbers connect side by side, they disappear and your spin counter resets.

Maths Spin: another number challenge

In Maths Spin, spin the sign wheel first, then spin the 10-section number wheel. The top arrow gives the first value and the bottom arrow gives the second. Press Show Solution when you want to see the formula and answer. Multiplication and division are available in Difficult Mode; division spins always give a whole-number answer. Answers stay within 1–6 for Simple and Moderate, and 1–9 for Difficult. You can use the same answer in multiple matching cells until a group clears.

For this game, < chooses the smaller wheel number and > chooses the larger. A result of zero cannot be placed until a matching zero is next to an empty cell, so spin a new pair if no matching space is available.
