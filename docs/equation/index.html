How to play

  1. Choose a modePick Simple, Moderate, or Difficult. Difficult mode adds multiplication and division.
  2. Spin once for both wheelsThe smaller sign wheel spins first. Once it stops, the larger number wheel spins automatically.
  3. Read the two numbersThe arrows point to the two values for your equation.
  4. Reveal and place the answerUse Show Solution when you want help, then click an empty space next to the same answer in its block.
  5. Complete the groupThe game fills connected empty spaces when there is enough room. Matching groups disappear and reset your spin counter.

Answers are kept within the mode’s number range. Division spins always make a whole-number result.
