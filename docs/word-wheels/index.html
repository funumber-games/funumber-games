    WORD WHEELS

    How to play

    1. Choose a modeSimple uses a 6 × 6 board with hints. Moderate uses an 8 × 8 board. Difficult uses a 9 × 9 board and a shorter timer.
    2. Spin the three rollersWhen they stop, your word appears and the timer starts. Numbers under the letters begin at 10 and drop every three seconds.
    3. Find the wordClick the three letters in order, or click and drag across them in a straight line. Find at least two of the four words before time runs out to continue.
    4. Score and keep goingThe revealed numbers add to each round total. Your round scores appear beside the board, and the game total builds across all 10 rounds.
